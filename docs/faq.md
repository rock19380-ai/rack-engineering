# FAQ

## Why not use a free paid-MCP template?

Templates solve wiring. RACK packages durable authority, retry/failure semantics, adversarial proof, recovery tooling, exact compatibility evidence, and upgrade requalification.

## Is Bazaar required?

No. Discovery metadata is non-authoritative and Bazaar is not a v1 core dependency.

## Are MPP sessions/subscriptions included?

No. Commercial v1 intentionally supports the qualified charge path only.
